Schedule

Eleven weeks, sixteen modules, fourteen labs. Everything for a week is due by the date shown.

For each week: read the modules on this site and run the simulations, complete the lab in NDG Online, then take the graded quiz in Canvas. Quiz numbers match module numbers. The course is fully online with no live sessions, so the dates are your only fixed points; most students do best by starting each week's reading by Thursday.

About the module and lab order

The course follows the EDU-210 curriculum, but User-ID comes earlier here (Module 9, alongside App-ID) than in EDU-210, where it is Module 12. Both features answer the same question, "what is this traffic and who sent it?", so they are easier to learn together. Lab numbers are always NDG Online's numbers, so in week 5 you do Lab 7 and then Lab 11, and Labs 8 to 10 follow in weeks 6 and 7. Each module page notes its EDU-210 counterpart.
